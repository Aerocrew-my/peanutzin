# Content and asset handover

Article image containers are editorial canvases, not mandatory photo containers.

Preferred order: genuine PEANUTZIN assets; authorized official event/promotional assets; client-provided assets; properly licensed imagery; original PEANUTZIN editorial graphics; branded graphic fallback.

Confirm copyright and usage scope, retain permission records outside the CMS, credit sources where appropriate, and never hotlink third-party images. Alt text should communicate useful visual content. Avoid AI-generated documentary-looking scenes that could be mistaken for real people or events.

The current responsive UI does not enforce one upload dimension or aspect ratio: article canvases vary by lead/card/mobile placement, while book canvases are taller in list/detail views. Supply high-resolution landscape editorial art and portrait book covers with generous safe margins; images use `object-fit: cover` and will crop differently. The generated OpenGraph image is exactly 1200×630. Uploads accept JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB.

The five Phase 7 graphics referenced by `202608260001_phase_7_editorial_artwork.sql` are development/editorial assets pending final client approval. Keep approved client originals in a separately backed-up library.
